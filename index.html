Whisker Lake

Pick a cat, row out onto the lake and drop your line. Fish, shrimp, jellyfish, sea turtles and lobsters live at different depths, and clams and oysters sit on the lake floor. The rarest catches hide deep down where it gets dark. Trash costs you points. Sharks eat fish, and sometimes one comes for your boat: row away fast or hide in the shallow water near the shore, or the trip is over. You can catch sharks too! Squids try to steal the fish on your hook, so reel in fast or row away. Find every creature in a place to open the trail to the next one on the map.

Each cat has a fishing talent. Choose one to set sail.
